02 The fix — the part nobody else ships

Our min-cut against the obvious alternative: delete a tool until the path breaks. “Workflows kept” counts the 30 tasks whose required tools still resolve.

strategypaths brokenworkflows keptgrants revokedsource

Revoking every grant is perfectly secure and perfectly useless — 0/30, because it breaks everything. That trade-off is the whole reason the fix needs a cost model rather than a rule.

05 Where we lose to published work

We re-implemented the baseline from Graph RAG-Tool Fusion (arXiv 2502.07223) on their public ToolLinkOS dataset, so our retrieval number can be compared to a real one.

We are below the paper. +8.9 mAP@10, not +71.7%. They tuned the retrieval task; we did not, because retrieval is not our product — we use it only to match a config to the catalog. We print the gap instead of dropping the row.
